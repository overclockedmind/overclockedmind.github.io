Solar Jetman Password Tool

Decode any 12-letter Solar Jetman (NES) password to see exactly what it holds, then change the lives, score, pod, and gear and get a new password with a correct checksum - one the game will accept.

Your password

The two low bits of the planet byte hold the map flags, and published sources disagree on which bit is which. Bisqwit's generator reads the high bit alone as Super; the published Super Mapping Device password sets both bits. In game, the Super device shows items and wormholes as flashing dots on the map.

How the password works

Twelve letters, each worth 4 bits from the alphabet BDGHKLMNPQRTVWXZ, pack into six bytes:

  • Lives - high half of byte 0 (0-15).
  • Score - six decimal digits, one per nibble, scattered across bytes 0, 1, 4, and 5.
  • Planet - top six bits of byte 2; the low two bits are the map flags.
  • Pod, shield, thrusters - packed into byte 4 alongside the score's hundreds digit.
  • Checksum - byte 3, computed from the other five bytes. Get it wrong and the game rejects the password, which is why hand-editing letters rarely works.
